

Paid Family Leave Version 1

Because New York has some of the strongest worker protection laws in the country, you likely have access to job-protected time off to care for your mother. If you drop everything and leave without notifying your employer or securing this leave, they could technically terminate you for job abandonment. You must formally request leave immediately.

Your Legal Protections in NYC

You have three primary safety nets that can protect your job while you are in Seattle. They generally require that your mother’s broken hip qualifies as a "serious health condition," which a major orthopedic injury requiring surgery or intensive rehabilitation absolutely does.


NY Paid Family Leave (PFL)
------------------------------
Job Fully Protected
Paid (Up to 67% of your average weekly wage for up to 12 weeks).

Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA)
------------------------------
Job Fully Protected
Unpaid

NYC Paid Safe and Sick Leave
------------------------------
Job Fully Protected


Immediate Steps You Must Take

Notify HR and your boss in writing immediately: Do not wait until you land in Seattle. Send an email explicitly stating that you are experiencing a family medical emergency because your mother has suffered a severe injury, and that you need to request family leave to care for her.

Request New York Paid Family Leave (PFL): If your employer is a private company, ask your HR department for the PFL request forms (specifically Form PFL-1 and Form PFL-3 for caregiving). You will need your mother's doctor in Seattle to fill out a portion confirming her medical status.

Ask for FMLA concurrently: If your company is large (50+ employees), ask for FMLA paperwork. FMLA and NY PFL usually run at the same time to give you maximum job protection.

Use your NYC Sick Time for the immediate transit days: Under the NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection (DCWP), you can legally use your accrued safe and sick leave to care for a family member. This can cover you for the next few days while your long-term PFL or FMLA paperwork is being processed.

Important Constraints to Keep in Mind

If your employer threatens termination or denies your legally protected leave, you can file an immediate complaint with the New York State Paid Family Leave helpline or call 311 to connect with the NYC DCWP.
